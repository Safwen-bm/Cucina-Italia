# Controluce

A landing page for **Controluce**, a fictional Italian restaurant built as a design piece, not a template.

The concept starts with one idea: *controluce* is Italian for **"backlit"** that moment when a room turns amber at the end of the day.

Controluce is imagined as a restaurant that **opens when the light turns amber and doesn't rush anyone home before it fades.**

Every design choice follows from that idea: deep bottle-green and marble-ivory instead of the usual cream-and-terracotta palette, brass details representing evening light, and the Italian flag appearing exactly once as a thin three-bar hairline rather than a background or loud accent.

## The idea behind it

The goal was to move as far as possible from the generic **"AI-made website"** aesthetic.

No stock hero + three-card layout.
No default color palette.
No filler copy.

Every section is designed to feel like a real restaurant website:

* The **menu** is structured like a printed menu card, with dishes, dotted leaders, and prices.
* The **gallery** uses an asymmetric photo grid instead of a uniform card layout.
* The **reviews** section is presented as a single pull quote rather than a testimonial carousel.
* The **story section**, dish names, and opening hours are written to feel specific to the restaurant rather than generic.

The result is a frontend-focused concept that puts **art direction, typography, layout, and visual identity** at the center.
